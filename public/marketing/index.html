    THE THINGS YOU’RE WONDERING

    A few good
    questions.

    Something specific about your setup?
    Just ask Reece

    Will CarpSync work with my existing website?+

    Yes. CarpSync can work alongside your existing website. We’ll look at the right booking connection for your site and explain what’s involved before setup.

    Can I take deposits and collect the balance later?+

    Yes. Deposits, full payments and staged balances can be tracked against the booking. The payment schedule should be configured around how your venue operates.

    Does it handle French lakes and exclusive weeks?+

    Yes. CarpSync supports weekly holiday bookings and exclusive lake hire as well as individual sessions. We’ll review arrival days, capacity and payment stages with you.

    Can anglers add bait and food when they book?+

    Yes. Your optional extras can be included in the booking journey, keeping the angler’s choices and the booking payment together.

    What about bookings I already have?+

    We’ll review your existing bookings, payments and availability as part of planning the move. The approach depends on your current system and the information you have, so we’ll agree that before a switch.

    Do I need a new website to use CarpSync?+

    No. You can discuss adding CarpSync to an existing website. If you want a full refresh, Karp Studio can also build a bespoke fishery website around the booking platform.

CarpSync / Getting started with CarpSync

Useful before the first conversation

A few details. A much better starting point.

You don’t need a technical brief. These practical notes help us understand what your fishery needs.

See it for my lake

1. Describe the sessions you sell

Write down the lakes and swims anglers can book, the usual session lengths and any fixed arrival or departure days. Add whether you sell individual places, exclusive lake hire or a mixture.

2. Explain how payments work

Tell us whether you take a deposit, how much it is, when the balance is due and what extras anglers can buy. Include the payment methods you need us to review.

3. Gather the existing bookings

Keep a record of confirmed dates, the customer attached to each booking and the payments already received. It helps plan a move without treating unpaid and confirmed bookings as the same thing.

4. Share your website and the sticking points

Send your website address and explain the jobs that take too much time: answering date enquiries, checking deposits, sending confirmations or tracking extras.

5. Ask to see your own routine

The useful question is how the system will handle your venue. Ask us to walk through the sessions, payment stages and customer journey that matter to you.

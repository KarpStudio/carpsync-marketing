The details that do the work

Every part of the booking, connected.

Practical tools for the jobs a fishery owner deals with every day.

See it for my lake

Live availability & booking rules

Keep the calendar organised around your venue.

  • Lakes, swims and exclusive hire
  • Session lengths and arrival days
  • Booking status and availability in one view

Deposits & outstanding balances

See the financial position of each booking.

  • Take a deposit or full payment
  • Configure staged balances
  • Track part-paid and outstanding amounts

Extras at booking

Let anglers organise more of their trip in the same journey.

  • Bait and particle packages
  • Food and accommodation options
  • Equipment or other venue upgrades

Customer records

Keep useful angler details with their bookings.

  • Booking history
  • Upcoming visits
  • One place to find customer information

Booking emails

Reduce repetitive messages around a booking.

  • Booking confirmations
  • Payment reminders
  • Information tied to the customer’s session

Mobile booking

Make the next session easy to arrange from a phone.

  • Clear dates and session selection
  • Extras in the booking journey
  • A straightforward route to payment

The right configuration matters

Features are configured around the venue. We’ll review your requirements and explain the setup before you commit, especially if you have unusual capacity rules, payment stages or an existing booking system to move from.
